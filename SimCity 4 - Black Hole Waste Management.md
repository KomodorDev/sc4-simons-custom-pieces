# Black Hole Waste Management

Installed on 16 September 2026 from the author’s Simtropolis ZIP. Installation and the rebuilt O-menu catalogue are verified. No building has been placed in a city by Codex.

## Place the building

1. Start **SimCity 4 Windows** and load the city that needs garbage disposal.
2. Press **O**, then select **Buildings & Lots**.
3. Click **Clear filters**, then type **Black Hole** in **Search buildings...**.
4. Select **Black Hole Waste Management v2**. Its included 3 × 4 lot is labelled **Black Hole Waste Management v3**; these names refer to the same supplied building.
5. Select that lot and click **Plop**.
6. Place it on a reasonably flat 3 × 4 area with its entrance along a road connected to the city. Resume the simulation to let it process waste.
