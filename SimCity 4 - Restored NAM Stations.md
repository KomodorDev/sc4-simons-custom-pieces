# SimCity 4 — restored NAM stations

Installed **14 September 2026** in your **SimCity 4 Windows** profile.

The separate **NAM Additional Transit Stations** package was absent from the Windows setup. It is now installed through sc4pac, with 59 station and transport files. The 138 previous package records are unchanged; the new total is 139 managed packages.

## Find the large HSR and elevated-rail hub

The likely station you remember is **HSRP Transit Hub by 3ddz**. It occupies **8 × 6 game squares** and connects high-speed rail, elevated rail, ordinary passenger rail, and buses.

1. Open **SimCity 4 Windows** and load a city.
2. Click **Transportation**.
3. Open **Miscellaneous Transportation** (also called **Misc-Transit**).
4. Open **Multi-modal Stations**. “Multi-modal” means the station connects several types of transport.
5. Hover over the station icons until the tooltip says **HSRP Transit Hub by 3ddz**.
6. Click that icon to show its placement preview.

An alternative is **Plop and Paint**: press **O**, select **Buildings & Lots**, and search for **HSRP** or **Transit Hub**. Select the hub and its lot, then choose **Plop**.

The package also restores additional bus, elevated-rail, monorail, HSR, hybrid-rail, railway, and multipurpose stations, plus transport transitions. These appear in their respective transport categories.
